Trends over time

Track a metric across every logged test date — team average, or individual athletes side by side.

Max is captured on test day with coaches using a Vertec. Jump avg is tested via OVR and doubles as the readiness signal: baseline is the average of every preseason test tagged as baseline (not just the first one). Status is On track at 95%+ of baseline, Monitor at 90–95%, Below baseline under 90% — matching the team spreadsheet exactly. Note that max and avg come from two different measurement tools, so they're not directly comparable to each other — see the Methodology tab for the full reasoning.
